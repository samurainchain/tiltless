Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

Short version: your journal lives in your browser. It leaves your device only if you sign in to sync it, and then it's stored only so you can use it. Tiltless itself has no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and we never sell your data. The exceptions you can opt into are the economic calendar from Investing.com and price charts from Binance (see below).

This policy covers the Tiltless web app at tiltless.xyz. Tiltless is run by an independent developer. You can reach us at tiltless.xyz@gmail.com.

What stays on your device

Without signing in, everything you enter stays in your browser's storage on your device: accounts, trades, notes, tags, rules, weekly reviews and screenshots you attach. We can't see it. Your settings (language, theme, selected account) are kept in the browser too.

When you fill in a trade from a screenshot, the image is read on your device. It is not uploaded for that.

What we collect, and why

  • Email address, if you sign in. We use it to send you sign-in links and to tie your synced journal to you. We don't send newsletters or marketing. Your account also keeps the app language you use, so those emails come in it.
  • Your journal, if you sign in and sync: the same data listed above, including attached screenshots, stored so it appears on your other devices and survives a cleared browser.
  • Feedback, if you send it through the form: your message, the type you picked, the app language and the screen you sent it from, plus your email if you're signed in or typed one in. We use it to improve Tiltless and to reply.
  • Partner program: if you open a partner's link, its code stays in your browser for up to 60 days; if you then create an account, we record which partner referred you. Partners see only how many people signed up and paid and what they earned, never who. If you become a partner, we store your partner code and the USDT payout address you enter.
  • Pro status: when you create an account we record a free Pro trial (7 days; 30 for accounts created by November 30, 2026); after that, until when Pro lasts and where it came from (trial, gift or purchase). The app keeps a copy in your browser so Pro works offline.
  • Purchases, if you buy Tiltless Pro: the plan, the price, the payment and order identifiers, the payment status and how long Pro lasts. We never see or store card numbers or wallet keys.

We don't use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies, and we don't build profiles of you (the optional Investing.com calendar below sets its own). The app stores a sign-in token in your browser so you stay signed in.

Who processes data for us

  • Supabase stores synced journals, sign-in accounts, feedback and purchase records, and runs the server functions.
  • Resend delivers sign-in emails.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the site. Like any web host, it may log technical data such as IP addresses.
  • NOWPayments processes crypto payments, if you buy Pro. Its own privacy policy applies to what you share with it while paying.
  • Telegram delivers messages from the Tiltless bot, if you connect it in Profile. We then store your Telegram chat id, time zone, language and which messages you want, and the bot reads your synced journal to send the day's result (in dollars, with the number of trades), a note when you reach a daily limit and the weekly review reminder. Telegram receives the text of these messages under its own privacy policy. Send /stop to the bot or press "Disconnect Telegram" and we delete the link.

Economic calendar. The Today screen can show Investing.com's economic calendar. It is off until you press "Show calendar", and that choice is remembered in your browser until you hide it. While it is on, the calendar loads from Investing.com in a frame, so Investing.com receives your IP address and browser details and may set its own cookies, including for advertising, under its own privacy policy. Tiltless sends it nothing from your journal.

Price charts. A crypto trade can show a price chart. It is off until you press "Show charts", and that choice is remembered in your browser until you hide it. While it is on, opening a crypto trade makes your browser request candles from Binance's public market data (data-api.binance.vision): Binance receives your IP address, the trading pair and the time span of the trade, under its own privacy policy. Prices, sizes, results and everything else in your journal are not sent.

These providers may process data outside your country. We share with them only what they need to do the job, and nothing else with anyone, unless the law requires it.

How long we keep it

Synced data stays until you ask us to delete your account. Feedback stays as long as it's useful for improving the app. Purchase records are kept as long as needed for accounting and the law.

Your choices and rights

  • Export: Profile → export a backup or a CSV of your trades at any time.
  • Delete from this browser: Profile → Delete all data.
  • Delete your account and synced data: email tiltless.xyz@gmail.com from the address you sign in with. We delete it within 30 days.
  • Access or correct: email us and we'll send you what we hold about you, or fix it.

Depending on where you live, you may have more rights under local law (for example, in the EU, the UK or California). Email us and we'll help.

Children

Tiltless is meant for adults. It isn't directed at anyone under 18, and we don't knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top. For significant changes, we'll also say so in the app.
