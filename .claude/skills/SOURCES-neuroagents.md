# Neuroagents (third-party)

`копирайтер/`, `маркетолог/` and `продажник/` are three of the "10 нейроагентов для Claude Code"
collected for the viewers of Artyom Voronkov's channel (t.me/artemvoronkov), received from the owner
as a zip on 2026-11-11 and copied unchanged. The pack has no formal license; its README says
"Пользуйся, меняй, передавай друзьям". Checked before adding: plain text instructions, no links but
Telegram and the author's channel, no hidden characters, nothing that runs or sends data.

Why these three: the Instagram skills (ig-*) are written for English captions, while Threads is in
Russian (/копирайтер, Telegram-style posts in the owner's voice); funnel math for Pro
(/маркетолог); conversations with bloggers and partners (/продажник). The other seven overlap with
what the repo already has (ig-reel, ig-caption, video skills, deep-research, the landing, the
Telegram bot).

For Tiltless, the product rules still apply over these files: no promises of profit or income,
demo numbers labelled, nothing posted or sent without the owner's yes.
